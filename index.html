What does it mean for a machine to truly understand?
This question has guided my journey through the world of Artificial Intelligence-from exploring how neural networks can learn complex patterns in my PhD research, to building intelligent systems that solve real-world challenges today. I am working at the intersection of AI research and industry innovation. My doctoral research focused on applied machine learning to resolve aircraft conflicts directly from trajectory data, pushing the boundaries of autonomous decision-making. Today, I lead applied AI/ML initiatives as an R&D Project Manager at Sogeti (Capgemini), transforming advances in Machine Learning, Deep Learning, LLMs, RAG, and Agentic AI into impactful solutions for enterprise clients. My mission is simple: to study intelligence by creating it to build AI systems that learn, reason, and act.
